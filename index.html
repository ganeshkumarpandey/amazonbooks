The Byline

"He writes for the people who stay curious — the ones who want to understand the invisible systems that run their world."

Research, writing, and the space between them

Rajeev Ranjan Pandey is the pen name of Ganesh Kumar Pandey, a PhD researcher at IIT Kharagpur, working at the intersection of electrical engineering, smart grid systems, and AI-driven power control. His research life — the late nights, the failed experiments, the slow victories — feeds directly into his writing.

He writes for students navigating academia, engineers curious about the systems around them, and anyone chasing something difficult but meaningful. His books strip away jargon and get straight to the truth, one short chapter at a time.
