About the Author

Research, writing, and the space between them

Rajeev Ranjan Pandey is a PhD researcher at IIT Kharagpur, working at the intersection of electrical engineering, smart grid systems, and AI-driven power control. His research life — the late nights, the failed experiments, the slow victories — feeds directly into his writing.

He writes for the people who stay curious: students navigating academia, engineers who want to understand the invisible systems around them, and anyone chasing something difficult but meaningful. His books strip away jargon and get to the truth.
