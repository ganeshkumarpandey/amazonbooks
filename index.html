About the Author

Research, writing,
and the space between them

Rajeev Ranjan Pandey is the pen name of a PhD researcher at IIT Kharagpur, working at the intersection of electrical engineering, smart grid systems, and AI-driven power control. His research life — the late nights, the failed experiments, the slow victories — feeds directly into his writing.

He writes for the people who stay curious: students navigating academia, engineers who want to understand the invisible systems around them, and anyone chasing something difficult but meaningful. His books strip away jargon and get to the truth.

Reader Favourite

The book PhD students pass around quietly

What Nobody Tells You About Getting a PhD began as an honest record of what graduate school actually feels like — not the polished version, but the real one. The Sunday-night anxiety. The departmental coffee. The strange emotional reality of spending years chasing answers that may or may not exist.

It found its readers because it told the truth. If you're in a PhD programme, or thinking about one, or you love someone who is — this is the book that finally says out loud what everyone already knows.
